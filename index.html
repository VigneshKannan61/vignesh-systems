The Missed Inbound Reality

Missed Calls Shouldn't Mean Missed Opportunities.

When patients research aesthetic treatments, they often call multiple practices. If an incoming call goes unanswered, they rarely leave voicemails—they move on to the next MedSpa.

The Everyday Challenge

Where Inbound Inquiries Fall Through

  • Incoming calls going unanswered during busy periods:

    Front-desk coordinators are often checking in arrivals, taking treatment payments, or assisting room providers, making it difficult to pick up incoming lines.

  • Potential patients not receiving timely responses:

    Prospective callers looking for Botox, dermal fillers, or laser consultations usually decide within minutes. A delayed response causes high drop-off.

  • Missed inquiries not receiving appropriate follow-up:

    Without an automated mechanism, staff must manually review call logs hours later or the next morning—long after the prospect has reached out elsewhere.
