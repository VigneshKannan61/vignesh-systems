#### Subsection 5B: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. **How does the AI receptionist handle complex medical questions?**
   - *Answer:* The AI handles administrative and operational inquiries—such as treatment overviews, hours, location, and consultation scheduling. For medical advice or custom clinical evaluations, it directs the caller to an in-person consultation or routes them to clinic staff.
2. **What happens if a caller has an urgent concern or insists on speaking with staff?**
   - *Answer:* If a caller requests a human team member or describes an urgent situation, the system flags the interaction and routes the call or notification directly to your designated staff number.
3. **Can this integrate with our current calendar and scheduling software?**
   - *Answer:* Yes. I connect the workflow directly to your existing booking calendar (such as Calendly, Boulevard, Acuity, or Google Calendar) via standard API or secure webhook integrations.
4. **How are telecommunication and SMS usage charges handled?**
   - *Answer:* Call minutes and outbound SMS messages run through transparent, direct-cost telephony providers (such as Twilio). You pay only for what your practice consumes at provider rates, with no hidden markup.
5. **How is patient inquiry information handled and kept secure?**
   - *Answer:* All captured data (caller contact numbers, requested treatment categories, timestamps) is transmitted directly to your secure CRM or designated inbox. No patient data is sold, repurposed, or retained on unnecessary third-party databases.
